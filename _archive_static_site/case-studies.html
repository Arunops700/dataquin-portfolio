CASE STUDY — 02

AI-Based Alerting Automation

Analysts were manually combing through multi-gigabyte transaction files to find suspicious activity — writing custom filter code for every request, waiting days per dataset, and missing patterns humans can't see. We built an automated intelligence pipeline that reads the data, learns what suspicious looks like, scores every transaction, and alerts in real time — with results in a live Power BI dashboard.

  • Handles massive datasets in minutes instead of days — and scales automatically
  • Catches hidden anomaly patterns that manual review misses
  • Runs on a schedule or in real time — always watching, never missing a case
  • Analysts focus on decisions, not data crunching

CASE STUDY — 03

EngageAI: Automated Client Activity Reporting

Client-facing teams were spending days manually reconstructing "what did we do this week?" from inboxes, call logs and calendars. EngageAI ingests sent mail, Teams call logs and calendar meetings through Power Automate, uses AI to generate "work performed" summaries, classifies each item by task type and billable status, and delivers a polished weekly report — automatically.

  • Comprehensive weekly summaries split by date and recipient
  • Automated task & billing breakdown — billable vs. non-billable
  • Call log analysis with duration, date and core call details
  • Reports turned around in under 3 hours, down from days of manual gathering

CASE STUDY — 04

Governed Reporting Modernization

A professional services firm's reporting estate had grown organically: Smartsheet reports here, local Excel files there, no central governance. Over a five-month engagement we migrated their reporting into a centralized workspace architecture, transitioned local Excel reporting to a SharePoint-based automated environment, and delivered governed Power BI dashboards with enterprise-grade privacy controls.

  • Smartsheet reports migrated into centralized workspace architecture
  • Local Excel reporting transitioned to SharePoint-based automation
  • Row-level security and governed data models aligned to firm standards
  • Future-ready connection architecture supporting scalable growth
